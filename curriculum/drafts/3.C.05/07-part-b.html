Part B practice — conceptual mastery

These original questions are designed for NET and State SET preparation; they are not previous-year questions.

1. Conceptual MCQ

Which molecular event determines the reading frame during ordinary cytosolic initiation?

  1. eIF4E binding the cap
  2. Stable initiator-tRNA pairing with a selected start codon in the P site
  3. Attachment of the poly(A) tail
  4. Binding of an elongator tRNA to the E site
Answer and explanation

B. Stable initiator-tRNA pairing with a selected start codon in the P site

The selected P-site triplet fixes the next A-site triplet and the downstream grouping. Cap recognition and polyadenylation facilitate expression but do not establish the frame; E is primarily an exit site.

2. Conceptual MCQ

The conventional eukaryotic initiator carries:

  1. N-formylmethionine in every compartment
  2. Unmodified methionine in the cytosolic pathway
  3. Leucine regardless of codon
  4. A dipeptide assembled before recruitment
Answer and explanation

B. Unmodified methionine in the cytosolic pathway

The usual nuclear-encoded cytosolic pathway uses Met-tRNAi. Formylmethionine is characteristic of typical bacteria and some organelle systems. The first peptide bond has not yet formed.

3. Conceptual MCQ

Which combination defines the ternary complex?

  1. eIF2, GDP and uncharged tRNA
  2. eIF4E, eIF4A and eIF4G
  3. eIF2, GTP and Met-tRNAi
  4. 40S, 60S and mRNA
Answer and explanation

C. eIF2, GTP and Met-tRNAi

The GTP-bound eIF2 delivers charged initiator tRNA. The second combination is eIF4F, while the fourth resembles ribosome components rather than the ternary complex.

4. Conceptual MCQ

Which factor facilitates GDP release and GTP rebinding to eIF2?

  1. eIF2B
  2. eIF5B
  3. eIF4A
  4. eIF1A
Answer and explanation

A. eIF2B

eIF2B is the nucleotide exchange factor for eIF2; it is not the joining GTPase eIF5B or the RNA helicase eIF4A. eIF1A functions on the small subunit.

5. Conceptual MCQ

What is the principal biochemical function of eIF4E?

  1. Recognition of the 5′ cap
  2. Hydrolysis of eIF2-bound GTP
  3. Methionine charging
  4. Peptide-bond formation
Answer and explanation

A. Recognition of the 5′ cap

eIF4E recognizes the methylated cap; eIF5 promotes the eIF2 GTPase step, methionyl-tRNA synthetase charges tRNA, and the large-subunit catalytic centre forms peptide bonds.

6. Conceptual MCQ

Which is not a core member of the standard eIF4F complex?

  1. eIF4E
  2. eIF4G
  3. eIF4A
  4. eIF4B
Answer and explanation

D. eIF4B

The three core members are E, G and A. eIF4B stimulates RNA-remodelling activity but is not one of the three defining components.

7. Conceptual MCQ

Which factor provides a scaffold linking cap recognition and RNA remodelling to recruitment?

  1. eIF5
  2. eIF4G
  3. eRF1
  4. eEF2
Answer and explanation

B. eIF4G

eIF4G connects initiation factors and, in the usual mammalian model, helps link eIF4F to eIF3. eRF1 and eEF2 operate later; eIF5 assists the eIF2 nucleotide transition.

8. Conceptual MCQ

A canonical 43S preinitiation complex contains which ribosomal particle?

  1. 60S alone
  2. 80S with peptidyl-tRNA
  3. 40S with initiation factors and ternary complex
  4. 30S with fMet-tRNA
Answer and explanation

C. 40S with initiation factors and ternary complex

The 43S assembly is prepared around the eukaryotic small subunit before stable start selection. The 60S joins later; 30S is bacterial.

9. Conceptual MCQ

Which feature distinguishes a start-selected 48S complex from an elongation-competent 80S complex?

  1. No initiator tRNA in 48S
  2. No mRNA in 48S
  3. No 60S subunit in 48S
  4. No start codon in 48S
Answer and explanation

C. No 60S subunit in 48S

The 48S complex already has mRNA and initiator tRNA positioned at the start but has not acquired the large subunit. The other options deny features of the start-selected state.

10. Conceptual MCQ

During scanning, which statement about the reading frame is most accurate?

  1. Every movement is obligatorily a full codon
  2. A stable protein-coding frame has not yet been established
  3. The large subunit synthesizes a leader peptide continuously
  4. The tRNA in the E site selects the start
Answer and explanation

B. A stable protein-coding frame has not yet been established

Scanning samples candidate positions before establishing a fixed reading frame. Three-nucleotide translocation and peptide synthesis describe elongation; the E site does not select the start.

11. Conceptual MCQ

For 5′-GCCAUGGCU-3′, which bases occupy −3 and +4 relative to the indicated AUG?

  1. G and G
  2. C and U
  3. A and G
  4. C and C
Answer and explanation

A. G and G

The A of AUG is +1. Three positions upstream is the first G, and one base after the AUG is G. The remaining options miscount the coordinates.

12. Conceptual MCQ

Which statement describes the Kozak context correctly?

  1. It is a DNA promoter
  2. It must always pair with 18S rRNA like a Shine–Dalgarno sequence
  3. It influences the probability of recognizing an encountered start codon
  4. It is a compulsory invariant ten-base sequence
Answer and explanation

C. It influences the probability of recognizing an encountered start codon

Flanking nucleotides affect start selection; the consensus describes favourable patterns rather than an absolute requirement. It is not a promoter or a bacterial-style pairing sequence.

13. Conceptual MCQ

Which factor is itself a joining GTPase in ordinary eukaryotic initiation?

  1. eIF5
  2. eIF5B
  3. eIF4E
  4. PABP
Answer and explanation

B. eIF5B

eIF5B acts during 60S joining and final maturation. eIF5 stimulates eIF2's GTPase reaction, eIF4E recognizes the cap, and PABP binds poly(A).

14. Conceptual MCQ

Where is the initiator tRNA immediately before the first elongator aminoacyl-tRNA arrives?

  1. E site
  2. A site
  3. P site
  4. Outside the ribosome on eIF2-GDP
Answer and explanation

C. P site

The initiator tRNA provides the donor from the P site. The A site is available for the next sense codon, while eIF2 has already relinquished the tRNA.

15. Conceptual MCQ

A scanning complex bypasses a weak upstream AUG without synthesizing any peptide. This is:

  1. Reinitiation
  2. Readthrough
  3. Leaky scanning
  4. Transcription attenuation
Answer and explanation

C. Leaky scanning

Leaky scanning means bypass before initiation. Reinitiation follows translation of an earlier ORF; readthrough concerns passage beyond a stop during translation.

16. Conceptual MCQ

Which statement about ATP hydrolysis during scanning is justified?

  1. Exactly one ATP per nucleotide for all mRNAs
  2. No ATP-dependent factor ever contributes
  3. ATP-dependent remodelling can assist structured leaders; cost is context dependent
  4. eIF4E hydrolyses ATP to recognize AUG
Answer and explanation

C. ATP-dependent remodelling can assist structured leaders; cost is context dependent

The structural demands and associated factors vary. eIF4A is an ATP-dependent RNA remodeler; eIF4E binds the cap and is not an AUG-reading ATPase.

17. Conceptual MCQ

What does a 48S toeprint principally report?

  1. Ribosome-associated obstruction to reverse transcription near a selected site
  2. The mass of the finished peptide
  3. The exact number of ATP molecules consumed
  4. The half-life of every mRNA
Answer and explanation

A. Ribosome-associated obstruction to reverse transcription near a selected site

Toeprinting maps a positional obstruction to primer extension. Appropriate RNA-only controls distinguish ribosome-associated stops from structural stops; it is not a direct peptide-output assay.

18. Conceptual MCQ

Which pair is evolutionarily related in the subunit-joining pathway?

  1. Bacterial IF2 and eukaryotic eIF2
  2. Bacterial IF2 and eukaryotic eIF5B
  3. Bacterial IF3 and eIF4E
  4. Bacterial RF1 and PABP
Answer and explanation

B. Bacterial IF2 and eukaryotic eIF5B

IF2 and eIF5B belong to a related initiation GTPase family. Eukaryotic eIF2 is a distinct ternary-complex factor despite the similar number.

19. Conceptual MCQ

Which observation most directly separates start recognition from successful protein synthesis?

  1. 48S at AUG persists but active 80S and peptide are absent
  2. A cap is present on RNA
  3. A poly(A) tail exists
  4. Total rRNA is abundant
Answer and explanation

A. 48S at AUG persists but active 80S and peptide are absent

A selected small-subunit intermediate demonstrates upstream recognition while the missing downstream output reveals a subsequent block. The other observations do not identify the stage.

20. Conceptual MCQ

Which statement about an IRES is most defensible?

  1. Every IRES requires every canonical cap factor
  2. Every cap-independent RNA contains an IRES
  3. An IRES can enable internal recruitment; factor requirements vary
  4. IRES translation has no reading frame
Answer and explanation

C. An IRES can enable internal recruitment; factor requirements vary

Internal entry is a route of recruitment, not a single fixed set of factors. Cap independence can also occur through other mechanisms, and productive decoding still requires a frame.

21. Conceptual MCQ

How does a closed-loop mRNA model relate the cap and tail?

  1. A new phosphodiester bond permanently circularizes the RNA
  2. Protein interactions involving eIF4G and PABP bring ends into functional proximity
  3. The tail base-pairs with the cap nucleotide
  4. PABP selects the start codon by anticodon pairing
Answer and explanation

B. Protein interactions involving eIF4G and PABP bring ends into functional proximity

The model concerns protein-mediated organization, not covalent closure or end-to-end base pairing. The initiating tRNA, not PABP, reads the start triplet.

22. Assertion–Reason

Assertion: eIF2 and eIF5B use separate nucleotide-dependent transitions. Reason: eIF2 delivers initiator tRNA, whereas eIF5B promotes subunit joining and maturation.

  1. Both true; reason explains assertion
  2. Both true; reason does not explain assertion
  3. Assertion true, reason false
  4. Assertion false, reason true
Answer and explanation

A. Both true; reason explains assertion

The two tasks are performed by separate GTPases in the standard pathway; the reason identifies why their nucleotide transitions are functionally distinct.

23. Assertion–Reason

Assertion: all eukaryotic mRNAs must have a poly(A) tail for initiation. Reason: PABP can enhance translation through interaction with eIF4G.

  1. Both true; reason explains assertion
  2. Both true; reason does not explain assertion
  3. Assertion true, reason false
  4. Assertion false, reason true
Answer and explanation

D. Assertion false, reason true

The interaction is a valid mechanism, but replication-dependent histone messages illustrate why the tail is not universally required.

24. Assertion–Reason

Assertion: a protein lacking N-terminal methionine could still have initiated with methionine. Reason: the initial residue may be removed during maturation.

  1. Both true; reason explains assertion
  2. Both true; reason does not explain assertion
  3. Assertion true, reason false
  4. Assertion false, reason true
Answer and explanation

A. Both true; reason explains assertion

The initiating residue and the mature protein's first residue need not be identical. The reason supplies the post-translational explanation.

25. Assertion–Reason

Assertion: every start AUG is used with identical efficiency. Reason: the −3 and +4 bases can influence start selection.

  1. Both true; reason explains assertion
  2. Both true; reason does not explain assertion
  3. Assertion true, reason false
  4. Assertion false, reason true
Answer and explanation

D. Assertion false, reason true

AUG identity alone does not guarantee equal selection. Context effects are precisely why the assertion fails.

26. Assertion–Reason

Assertion: eIF2B is an exchange factor rather than a kinase that phosphorylates bound GDP. Reason: GDP dissociation permits a separate GTP molecule to bind.

  1. Both true; reason explains assertion
  2. Both true; reason does not explain assertion
  3. Assertion true, reason false
  4. Assertion false, reason true
Answer and explanation

A. Both true; reason explains assertion

Exchange changes nucleotide occupancy without direct phosphorylation of the original bound GDP.

27. Multiple statement

Identify the correct statements: I. eIF4A is an ATP-dependent RNA-remodelling factor. II. eIF5 stimulates eIF2's GTPase reaction. III. eIF2B is a 60S structural rRNA. IV. eIF4E recognizes the cap.

  1. I and III only
  2. I, II and IV only
  3. II and III only
  4. All four
Answer and explanation

B. I, II and IV only

I, II and IV correctly describe factor roles; III confuses a soluble exchange factor with ribosomal RNA.

28. Multiple statement

Which statements about canonical initiation are true? I. 40S and 60S form an 80S particle. II. Svedberg units are additive. III. Initiator tRNA occupies P at the end of initiation. IV. eIF4B is a core member of eIF4F.

  1. I and III
  2. II and IV
  3. I, II and III
  4. III and IV
Answer and explanation

A. I and III

I and III are correct. Sedimentation values are not arithmetic sums, and eIF4B is auxiliary rather than a defining eIF4F member.

29. Matching

Match A=eIF4E, B=eIF2B, C=eIF5B, D=eIF1 with 1=start-selection fidelity, 2=cap binding, 3=nucleotide exchange, 4=subunit joining.

  1. A2 B3 C4 D1
  2. A1 B4 C2 D3
  3. A3 B2 C1 D4
  4. A4 B1 C3 D2
Answer and explanation

A. A2 B3 C4 D1

The distinctive assignments are cap binding, exchange, joining and fidelity, respectively. The other options mix steps of the pathway.

30. Matching

Match A=43S, B=start-selected 48S, C=80S initiation complex, D=eIF4F with 1=cap-associated factor complex, 2=prepared small subunit, 3=small subunit at start, 4=joined initiation ribosome.

  1. A1 B2 C3 D4
  2. A2 B3 C4 D1
  3. A3 B4 C1 D2
  4. A4 B1 C2 D3
Answer and explanation

B. A2 B3 C4 D1

The states progress from prepared small subunit to selected start to joined ribosome. eIF4F is a separate factor complex rather than a ribosomal particle.
