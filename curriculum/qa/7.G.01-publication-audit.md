## Syllabus and scope
Both CSIR-HRDG Life Sciences syllabi (29 Jan 2026 and revised merged 21 Jan 2026), and archived MP SET Life Sciences syllabus were consulted. This node supplies the comfort-zone foundation: temperature compartments and rhythms; ectothermy/endothermy versus homeothermy/poikilothermy and heterothermy; thermal preference versus thermoneutrality; LCT/UCT, heat balance and metabolic curves; Q10, thermal performance, experimental evidence and tolerance. Physical, chemical, neural regulation and acclimatization receive separate treatments in 7.G.02–05. Numerical datasets are explicitly hypothetical, not published measurements.
