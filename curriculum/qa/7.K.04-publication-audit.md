## Scientific review (pass 1)

Internal review: definitions and verification limits; controlled colonisation, transfer, rescue and causal inference; intestinal morphology, mucosal immunity, metabolism and developmental confounding; evidence from selected primary records and reviews; all 52 answer explanations and worked numerical examples. Landmark examples include Bäckhed, Sudo, Stappenbeck, Ivanov, Smith, Mazmanian and Turnbaugh. Avoided universal claims from mouse studies; no claim of absolute sterility from negative testing. Numerical examples and keys reviewed: energy-balance differences, 0.5 and 1.25 rescue ratios, additive interaction 6, 75% relative increase and 175%/155% cell-count comparisons.
