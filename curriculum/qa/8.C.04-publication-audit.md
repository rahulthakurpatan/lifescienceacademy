## Scope and coverage

- Parental polymorphism and founder selection (`founders`): Informative markers, genetic contrast, crossing plan and segregation assumptions.
- F1 and F2 populations (`f2`): Single-locus and linked two-locus genotype frequencies, dominance and information content.
- Backcross populations (`bc`): Gamete scoring, background recovery, linkage drag and selection.
- Recombinant inbred lines (`ril`): Fixation, selfing recurrence, recombinant probability derivation and mapping conversion.
- Doubled haploid populations (`dh`): Haploid origin, doubling, one-meiosis information and distortion.
- Near-isogenic lines (`nil`): Introgression, residual donor segments, causal controls and validation.
- Advanced intercross populations (`advanced`): Accumulated recombination, independent information and intermated maize evidence.
- Population size, marker density and mapping resolution (`resolution`): Binomial detection, upper limits, quantitative design comparison.
- Phenotype replication and environmental control (`phenotype`): Experimental units, confounding, segregation distortion and line means.
- Selection of mapping population for a biological question (`choice`): Comparative design, worked crop case, assumptions and practical trade-offs.
