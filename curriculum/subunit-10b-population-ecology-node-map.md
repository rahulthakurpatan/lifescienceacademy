## Subunit contract

Understand how populations are organised, measured and regulated, using demographic information, mathematical growth models, life history strategies and spatial population dynamics to explain persistence and extinction.

**Planned nodes:** 7. See [Unit 10 master map](unit-10-ecology-behavioural-biology-subunit-map.md) for the full syllabus and legacy crosswalk.

## Node contracts

### 10.B.01 — Population Characteristics and Estimation Methods

**Student-facing description:** Measure population size, density, abundance, natality, mortality, migration and dispersion using quadrats, transects and mark–recapture approaches.

**Core scope:** Population boundaries, density versus abundance, absolute and relative estimates, birth/death and immigration/emigration accounting, clumped/random/uniform dispersion, quadrat and transect sampling, detection bias, Lincoln–Petersen and Chapman estimators, closure and equal catchability assumptions, sampling error and representative field methods.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Calculate density and mark–recapture estimates, interpret spatial patterns and identify violated sampling assumptions.

**Progression:** Build on the preceding ecological subunits and relevant earlier units. Prepare 10.B.02.

### 10.B.02 — Population Growth Models and Carrying Capacity

**Student-facing description:** Compare geometric, exponential and logistic growth, intrinsic and finite rates of increase, doubling time, carrying capacity and mathematical population projections.

**Core scope:** Discrete N(t)=N0 lambda^t, continuous dN/dt=rN and N(t)=N0 exp(rt), r versus lambda and units, doubling time, logistic dN/dt=rN(1-N/K), carrying capacity as context-dependent, maximum absolute growth at K/2 in the simple model, assumptions and limitations, growth curves and population forecasts.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Solve growth and doubling-time problems, interpret per-capita versus total growth curves and compare exponential/logistic predictions.

**Progression:** Build on 10.B.01. Prepare 10.B.03.

### 10.B.03 — Population Regulation and Dynamic Fluctuations

**Student-facing description:** Explain density-dependent and density-independent effects, delayed feedback, environmental variability, population cycles, overshooting, crashes and Allee effects.

**Core scope:** Resource limitation, competition and disease as possible density-dependent effects, weather and catastrophes, regulation versus limitation, delayed density dependence, discrete logistic dynamics and cycles, overshoot and collapse, demographic/environmental variability, positive density dependence and strong/weak Allee effects; distinguish models from universal outcomes.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret time series and per-capita growth curves, identify stable/unstable equilibria and compare delayed feedback with external forcing.

**Progression:** Build on 10.B.02. Prepare 10.B.04.

### 10.B.04 — Life Tables, Survivorship and Reproductive Parameters

**Student-facing description:** Construct cohort and static life tables, comparing survival, mortality, fecundity, survivorship curves, life expectancy, net reproductive rate and generation time.

**Core scope:** Age classes, lx, dx, qx, mx, survivorship types I–III and intermediate patterns, cohort versus time-specific tables, age-specific fecundity, R0=sum(lx mx), generation-time approximations and Euler–Lotka concept, life expectancy from person-time, assumptions and units; distinguish R0 from instantaneous growth.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Complete life tables, calculate R0 and generation time, compare survival/fecundity schedules and interpret survivorship curves.

**Progression:** Build on 10.B.03. Prepare 10.B.05.

### 10.B.05 — Age Structure, Sex Ratio and Population Projection

**Student-facing description:** Relate age pyramids, sex ratios and reproductive value to age-structured models, Leslie matrices and long-term demographic projections.

**Core scope:** Age and stage structure, expanding/stable/declining pyramids with contextual caveats, primary/secondary/adult sex ratios, fertility and survival entries of Leslie matrices, projection vector and dominant eigenvalue as long-term multiplier, stable age distribution and reproductive value, stage-structured Lefkovitch context and limitations.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Perform small matrix projections, interpret demographic pyramids and compare short-term trends with asymptotic growth.

**Progression:** Build on 10.B.04. Prepare 10.B.06.

### 10.B.06 — Life History Strategies and Evolutionary Trade-offs

**Student-facing description:** Compare classical r/K models with modern life history theory, reproductive timing, semelparity, iteroparity, offspring size–number trade-offs and allocation.

**Core scope:** Age and size at maturity, fecundity, survival, longevity, reproductive effort, current versus future reproduction, offspring quality/quantity, semelparity and iteroparity, classical r/K-selection as historical heuristic not rigid species categories, environmental predictability, mortality schedules and evolutionary trade-offs.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret allocation and fitness data, compare life-history strategies under different survival regimes and critique simplistic r/K classifications.

**Progression:** Build on 10.B.05. Prepare 10.B.07.

### 10.B.07 — Metapopulation Dynamics and Spatial Population Ecology

**Student-facing description:** Connect demes and habitat patches with colonisation, local extinction, dispersal, Levins models, source–sink dynamics, rescue effects and landscape connectivity.

**Core scope:** Demes, patch occupancy, interdemic extinction, colonisation, Levins dp/dt=cp(1-p)-ep and equilibrium under assumptions, patch quality/size and isolation, source/sink distinctions, rescue effect, spatial synchrony, dispersal and corridors, fragmentation and extinction risk; conservation applications 10.F.04–05.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Calculate equilibrium occupancy and threshold conditions, interpret patch networks and distinguish source–sink dynamics from simple occupancy.

**Progression:** Build on 10.B.06. Bridge to subunit 10.C.
