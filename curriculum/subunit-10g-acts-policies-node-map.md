## Subunit contract

Examine agricultural biodiversity and plant genetic resources through germplasm conservation, climate-resilient strategies, international treaties, Indian biodiversity legislation and the protection of plant varieties and farmers' rights.

**Planned nodes:** 5. See [Unit 10 master map](unit-10-ecology-behavioural-biology-subunit-map.md) for the full syllabus and legacy crosswalk.

## Node contracts

### 10.G.01 — Agricultural Biodiversity and Plant Genetic Resources

**Student-facing description:** Relate crop diversity, Vavilov's centres of origin and diversity, landraces, crop wild relatives and germplasm to genetic erosion, sustainable agriculture and food security.

**Core scope:** Agrobiodiversity at genetic/species/agroecosystem levels, PGRFA scope, landraces, traditional and improved varieties, crop wild relatives and gene pools, centres of origin versus diversity and modern evidence, Vavilov historical framework, domestication and genetic bottlenecks, genetic erosion, farmer-maintained diversity, resilience and food security, representative Indian examples.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare germplasm categories and diversity data, distinguish centre of origin from centre of diversity and explain trade-offs in crop uniformity.

**Progression:** Build on the preceding ecological subunits and relevant earlier units. Prepare 10.G.02.

### 10.G.02 — Seed Gene Banks and Climate-Resilient Germplasm Conservation

**Student-facing description:** Trace germplasm exploration, accessions, seed viability, base and active collections, regeneration, field and in-vitro banks, cryopreservation and climate-resilient conservation.

**Core scope:** Collection and accession documentation, passport and characterisation data, orthodox, intermediate and recalcitrant seed behaviour, viability and genetic integrity, base/active collections, regeneration and sampling bottlenecks, field banks, in-vitro and cryopreservation principles, safety duplication, NBPGR and Svalbard Global Seed Vault as distinct institutions/functions, climate threats to PGR and complementary in-situ/on-farm/ex-situ strategies; no operational storage protocol.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret seed viability trends and accession records, compare conservation strategies for contrasting seed types and assess climate risk to genetic resources.

**Progression:** Build on 10.G.01. Prepare 10.G.03.

### 10.G.03 — International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture

**Student-facing description:** Explain ITPGRFA conservation and sustainable-use objectives, the Multilateral System, Annex I crops, SMTA, access and benefit sharing, Farmers' Rights and germplasm exchange.

**Core scope:** Treaty scope, conservation and sustainable use, Multilateral System and Annex I crop/forage coverage subject to treaty conditions, Standard Material Transfer Agreement, facilitated access and benefit-sharing mechanisms, Farmers' Rights under Article 9 and national implementation, distinction from CBD/Nagoya bilateral access frameworks and PPV&FR domestic law; verify authoritative treaty text and decisions when teaching.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare conceptual germplasm-access cases under stated treaty conditions, distinguish MLS from universal free access and identify benefit-sharing objectives.

**Progression:** Build on 10.G.02. Prepare 10.G.04.

### 10.G.04 — Biological Diversity Act 2002 and Biodiversity Governance in India

**Student-facing description:** Examine India's Biological Diversity Act, subsequent amendments and rules, NBA, SBBs, BMCs, People's Biodiversity Registers, access and benefit sharing and traditional knowledge.

**Core scope:** Objectives, institutional tiers and jurisdiction, National Biodiversity Authority, State Biodiversity Boards and Biodiversity Management Committees, People's Biodiversity Registers, conservation and sustainable use, access and benefit-sharing framework, traditional knowledge, relevant approval/intimation concepts and distinctions, 2023 amendment and subsequent rules/regulations as version-controlled research requirements; use India Code, Gazette and NBA primary documents at lesson publication, no assumption of current legal wording or individual legal advice.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Match institutional functions to hypothetical cases using dated statutory sources, compare access/benefit-sharing principles and distinguish Act, rules and regulations.

**Progression:** Build on 10.G.03. Prepare 10.G.05.

### 10.G.05 — Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights and Integrated Conservation Policies

**Student-facing description:** Compare breeders', farmers' and researchers' rights under the PPV&FR framework, plant variety registration, DUS criteria, benefit sharing and the National Gene Fund.

**Core scope:** PPV&FR Act 2001 objectives, authority, categories including new, extant, farmers' and essentially derived varieties where applicable, novelty and DUS with category-specific conditions, registration principles, breeders' rights, farmers' rights and exceptions, researchers' rights, benefit sharing, National Gene Fund, relationship to biodiversity and PGRFA policy; verify official Act/rules, authority guidance and amendments when developing lesson; no legal advice.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare variety categories and DUS evidence, distinguish rights and exceptions in hypothetical examples grounded in dated official sources and integrate national/international policy.

**Progression:** Build on 10.G.04. Bridge to subunit 10.H.
