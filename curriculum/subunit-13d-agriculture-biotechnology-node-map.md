## Subunit contract

Link plant culture, transformation, agricultural genomics, molecular diagnostics and marker-assisted crop improvement with environmental evaluation.

**Planned nodes:** 7. See [master map](unit-13-advances-in-biotechnology-subunit-map.md) for syllabus and legacy traceability.

## Node contracts

### 13.D.01 — Plant Tissue and Cell Culture Biotechnology

**Student-facing description:** Totipotency, explants, callus, organogenesis, somatic embryogenesis, micropropagation, meristem culture, haploids and doubled haploids, protoplast and somatic hybridisation concepts.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare developmental routes and interpret hypothetical regeneration and genetic stability evidence.

**Progression:** Build on preceding relevant units and the subunit's prerequisite concepts. Prepare 13.D.02.

### 13.D.02 — Transgenic Plants and Plant Molecular Farming

**Student-facing description:** Genetic transformation and transgenic plant concepts, Agrobacterium-mediated and physical delivery categories, trait validation, representative GE crop traits, plantibodies and molecular farming.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare conceptual transformation and product categories, evaluate published benign trait evidence without protocols.

**Progression:** Build on 13.D.01. Prepare 13.D.03.

### 13.D.03 — Agricultural Genomics and Expressed Sequence Tags

Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

### 13.D.04 — Molecular Diagnosis, Strain Identification and Plant Genotyping

**Student-facing description:** Molecular approaches to crop disease diagnosis and strain identification, RFLP, RAPD, AFLP, SSR and SNP markers, non-gel techniques including SNP arrays, KASP and sequencing-based genotyping.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. ### 13.D.06 — Foreground and Background Selection, Introgression and Pyramiding

**Student-facing description:** Marker-assisted backcrossing concepts, foreground and background selection, recombinant selection, linkage drag, introgression, gene pyramiding and multiple-trait selection.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

## Editorial and progression contract

- Curriculum architecture only; no unpublished lesson URLs, sitemap edits or lesson-completion counter changes.
- Original friendly academic English, BSc foundations to MSc/NET/SET depth, connected narratives, original diagrams, classical evidence, modern applications, quantitative examples with assumptions and units, misconception checks, Part B and analytical Part C practice with solutions.
- Unit 13 owns biotechnology application and conceptual comparison; Unit 14 owns measurement principles, experimental evidence, instrumentation, statistical interpretation and ethical governance. Unit 3 owns molecular mechanisms, Unit 4 immunology, Unit 5 development, Unit 8 inheritance, Unit 10 ecology, Unit 11 algorithms and Unit 12 industrial engineering. Preview later methods when needed without duplicating full chapters.
- Use benign educational examples, abstract models and published high-level evidence. No actionable pathogen culture or engineering, harmful genetic modification, production recipes, invasive experimentation, radioisotope handling or medical treatment instructions. Research involving biological hazards, animals, humans or radiation requires trained personnel and authorised oversight.
- Verify current laws, biosafety rules, regulatory bodies and journal AI policies at actual publication; distinguish prediction from experimental or clinical validation. Maintain provenance and research integrity.
