## Subunit contract

Progress from electromagnetic and optical measurements to structural determination, mass analysis and biomolecular interaction measurements.

**Planned nodes:** 7. See [master map](unit-14-methods-in-biology-subunit-map.md) for syllabus and legacy traceability.

## Node contracts

### 14.B.01 — Spectroscopy Foundations and UV–Visible Analysis

**Student-facing description:** Electromagnetic radiation, absorption, Beer–Lambert law, extinction coefficient, spectra, calibration, scattering interference, instrumentation and limitations.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Calculate absorbance and concentration from supplied data and evaluate linearity and blanks.

**Progression:** Build on preceding relevant units and the subunit's prerequisite concepts. Prepare 14.B.02.

### 14.B.02 — Fluorescence Spectroscopy and Circular Dichroism

**Student-facing description:** Excitation/emission, Stokes shift, quantum yield, quenching, FRET, anisotropy, circular dichroism and protein conformational analysis.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret fluorescence and CD spectra, compare relative changes and distinguish signal from structural proof.

**Progression:** Build on 14.B.01. Prepare 14.B.03.

### 14.B.03 — NMR and ESR Spectroscopy

Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

### 14.B.04 — X-Ray Diffraction and Molecular Structure Determination

### 14.B.05 — Cryo-Electron Microscopy and Integrated Structural Analysis

**Student-facing description:** Cryo-EM single-particle imaging, projection and reconstruction concepts, maps, resolution, heterogeneity, NMR structural restraints and comparison of X-ray, NMR and cryo-EM.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. ### 14.B.06 — Mass Spectrometry and Molecular Identification

**Student-facing description:** Mass-to-charge ratio, ionisation concepts including ESI and MALDI, mass analysers, isotope patterns, tandem MS, peptide and metabolite identification, quantitative limitations.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations.
