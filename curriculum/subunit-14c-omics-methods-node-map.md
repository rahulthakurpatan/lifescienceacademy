## Subunit contract

Connect genome-level measurements, molecular identity, expression, protein interactions, metabolic profiling and single-cell datasets with integrated experimental inference.

**Planned nodes:** 7. See [master map](unit-14-methods-in-biology-subunit-map.md) for syllabus and legacy traceability.

## Node contracts

### 14.C.01 — Genome Organisation and Comparative Genomics

**Student-facing description:** Prokaryotic and eukaryotic genome organisation, sequencing coverage, genome assembly and annotation evidence, comparative genomics, synteny and structural variation.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare schematic genome maps and supplied coverage and assembly metrics; algorithms in Unit 11.

**Progression:** Build on preceding relevant units and the subunit's prerequisite concepts. Prepare 14.C.02.

### 14.C.02 — DNA Fingerprinting, Molecular Markers and DNA Barcoding

**Student-facing description:** RFLP, RAPD, AFLP, STR and SNP profiles, DNA fingerprinting applications, DNA barcoding, reference libraries and evidence limitations.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret illustrative genetic profiles and barcode matches; distinguish a match from a unique identification claim.

**Progression:** Build on 14.C.01. Prepare 14.C.03.

### 14.C.03 — Global Gene Expression and Comparative Transcriptomics

**Student-facing description:** Microarray and RNA-seq principles, transcript quantification, differential gene expression, fold change, normalisation, biological replication, multiple testing and comparative transcriptomics.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret expression matrices, volcano plots and validation controls without equating significance with biological effect.

**Progression:** Build on 14.C.02. Prepare 14.C.04.

### 14.C.06 — Targeted and Untargeted Metabolomics

**Student-facing description:** Metabolic profiling, targeted versus untargeted measurement, LC/GC-MS and NMR, internal standards, feature annotation, pathway interpretation and metabolic flux distinction.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.
