## Subunit contract

Understand biological tracer measurements, isotope detection, interpretation, molecular imaging and radiation protection in authorised research settings.

**Planned nodes:** 5. See [master map](unit-14-methods-in-biology-subunit-map.md) for syllabus and legacy traceability.

## Node contracts

### 14.D.01 — Radioisotopes and Principles of Biological Tracing

**Student-facing description:** Isotopes, radioactive decay, alpha, beta and gamma emissions, half-life, activity units, specific activity and tracer versus stable isotope concepts.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Solve abstract decay and unit-conversion examples without radioactive-material handling.

**Progression:** Build on preceding relevant units and the subunit's prerequisite concepts. Prepare 14.D.02.

### 14.D.02 — Radiation Detection and Quantitative Measurement

**Student-facing description:** Geiger–Müller and scintillation detection principles, counting efficiency, background, quenching, count statistics and measurement uncertainty.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret fictional count data and distinguish measured counts from activity.

**Progression:** Build on 14.D.01. Prepare 14.D.03.

### 14.D.03 — Radiolabel Incorporation and Biological Tracer Analysis

**Student-facing description:** Conceptual incorporation of labels in biological molecules, cells and tissues, pulse–chase logic, turnover, distribution and metabolic tracing.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret published schematic pulse–chase and tracer curves; no labelling procedure or isotope sourcing.

**Progression:** Build on 14.D.02. Prepare 14.D.04.

### 14.D.04 — Autoradiography and Molecular Imaging

**Student-facing description:** Autoradiographic localisation, spatial resolution, radiotracer imaging, PET and SPECT principles, signal interpretation and limitations.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare example images and interpret signal distribution versus molecular abundance.

**Progression:** Build on 14.D.03. Prepare 14.D.05.

### 14.D.05 — Radiation Safety and Responsible Radioisotope Research

**Student-facing description:** Ionising radiation hazards, exposure versus contamination, ALARA, time/distance/shielding concepts, dosimetry, trained personnel, regulatory oversight and authorised waste management.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Evaluate hypothetical safety governance cases and recognise when specialist radiation safety review is required.

**Progression:** Build on 14.D.04. Bridge to subunit 14.E.

## Editorial and progression contract

- Curriculum architecture only; no unpublished lesson URLs, sitemap edits or lesson-completion counter changes.
- Original friendly academic English, BSc foundations to MSc/NET/SET depth, connected narratives, original diagrams, classical evidence, modern applications, quantitative examples with assumptions and units, misconception checks, Part B and analytical Part C practice with solutions.
- Unit 13 owns biotechnology application and conceptual comparison; Unit 14 owns measurement principles, experimental evidence, instrumentation, statistical interpretation and ethical governance. Unit 3 owns molecular mechanisms, Unit 4 immunology, Unit 5 development, Unit 8 inheritance, Unit 10 ecology, Unit 11 algorithms and Unit 12 industrial engineering. Preview later methods when needed without duplicating full chapters.
- Use benign educational examples, abstract models and published high-level evidence. No actionable pathogen culture or engineering, harmful genetic modification, production recipes, invasive experimentation, radioisotope handling or medical treatment instructions. Research involving biological hazards, animals, humans or radiation requires trained personnel and authorised oversight.
- Verify current laws, biosafety rules, regulatory bodies and journal AI policies at actual publication; distinguish prediction from experimental or clinical validation. Maintain provenance and research integrity.
