## Subunit contract

Connect antibody and staining principles with quantitative detection, protein identification, cell profiling and spatial localisation.

**Planned nodes:** 6. See [master map](unit-14-methods-in-biology-subunit-map.md) for syllabus and legacy traceability.

## Node contracts

### 14.E.01 — Histochemistry and Antibody Generation

**Student-facing description:** Histochemical stains, antigen recognition, polyclonal and monoclonal antibody generation concepts, hybridomas, antibody specificity, affinity and validation.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare staining and antibody controls and distinguish specific signal from background.

**Progression:** Build on preceding relevant units and the subunit's prerequisite concepts. Prepare 14.E.02.

### 14.E.02 — ELISA, Radioimmunoassay and Quantitative Immunodetection

**Student-facing description:** Direct, indirect, sandwich and competitive ELISA, RIA concepts, standard curves, assay range, cross-reactivity, sensitivity and specificity.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret fictional assay curves and calculate concentrations within validated ranges.

**Progression:** Build on 14.E.01. Prepare 14.E.03.

### 14.E.03 — Western Blotting and Immunoprecipitation

Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Progression:** Build on 14.E.02. Prepare 14.E.04.

### 14.E.06 — In Situ Hybridisation, FISH and GISH

Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Progression:** Build on 14.E.05. Bridge to subunit 14.F.

## Editorial and progression contract

Preview later methods when needed without duplicating full chapters.
