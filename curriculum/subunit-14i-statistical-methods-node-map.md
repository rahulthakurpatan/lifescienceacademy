## Subunit contract

Build statistical reasoning from measurement quality and probability to sampling, inference, association, comparative tests and multivariate analysis.

**Planned nodes:** 7. See [master map](unit-14-methods-in-biology-subunit-map.md) for syllabus and legacy traceability.

## Node contracts

### 14.I.01 — Measurement Quality, Experimental Error and Descriptive Statistics

**Student-facing description:** Accuracy, precision, calibration, systematic/random error, signal-to-noise ratio, mean, median, mode, range, variance, standard deviation, IQR and coefficient of variation.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Calculate summary measures, compare measurement series and identify outliers without arbitrary deletion.

**Progression:** Build on preceding relevant units and the subunit's prerequisite concepts. Prepare 14.I.02.
