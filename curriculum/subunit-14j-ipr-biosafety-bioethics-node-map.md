## Subunit contract

Connect intellectual property, biological safety, animal and human research ethics, publication integrity and responsible AI use to scientific practice.

**Planned nodes:** 5. See [master map](unit-14-methods-in-biology-subunit-map.md) for syllabus and legacy traceability.

## Node contracts

### 14.J.01 — Intellectual Property Rights and Patent Information

**Student-facing description:** Patents, copyright, trademarks, designs, trade secrets and plant variety protection, novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability, patent databases including Indian Patent Advanced Search, WIPO PATENTSCOPE and Espacenet.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Read illustrative patent records, distinguish IP categories and verify jurisdiction-specific rules from official sources.

**Progression:** Build on preceding relevant units and the subunit's prerequisite concepts. Prepare 14.J.02.

### 14.J.02 — Biosafety, Containment and Regulatory Governance

**Student-facing description:** Biological risk assessment, biological safety measures, biosafety levels BSL-1–4, containment principles, institutional oversight, Indian DBT biosafety framework and relevant regulatory guidelines, dual-use awareness.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare risk-assessment and governance scenarios without laboratory access, containment design or pathogen handling instructions.

**Progression:** Build on 14.J.01. Prepare 14.J.03.

### 14.J.03 — Animal Ethics and Responsible Biomedical Research

**Student-facing description:** 3Rs, animal welfare, ethical review, humane endpoints as governance concepts, CCSEA and IAEC context, informed consent, human research ethics, vulnerable participants and privacy.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Evaluate hypothetical ethical-review scenarios and identify required oversight, consent and welfare considerations.

**Progression:** Build on 14.J.02. Prepare 14.J.04.

### 14.J.04 — Research Integrity, Publication Ethics and Plagiarism

**Student-facing description:** Study design integrity, falsification, fabrication, plagiarism, citation, authorship, conflicts, peer review, duplicate publication, corrections, retractions and data management.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Identify integrity problems in fictional cases and distinguish legitimate paraphrasing and image processing from misconduct.

**Progression:** Build on 14.J.03. Prepare 14.J.05.

### 14.J.05 — Responsible Use of AI and Reproducible Scientific Practice

**Student-facing description:** AI in research and publication, hallucinations, disclosure, verification, authorship limitations, confidentiality, bias, reproducibility, data and code provenance and journal policies.

**Core scope:** Develop each concept in the description as an internal section, beginning with definitions and prerequisites, then mechanism or measurement principle, comparison, applications, controls, assumptions and limitations. Connect the topics using a representative benign example and evidence appropriate to the node.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Audit fictional AI-assisted research outputs for source verification, privacy, disclosure and reproducibility.

**Progression:** Build on 14.J.04. Integrate research methods and conclude the curriculum.

## Editorial and progression contract

- Curriculum architecture only; no unpublished lesson URLs, sitemap edits or lesson-completion counter changes.
- Original friendly academic English, BSc foundations to MSc/NET/SET depth, connected narratives, original diagrams, classical evidence, modern applications, quantitative examples with assumptions and units, misconception checks, Part B and analytical Part C practice with solutions.
- Unit 13 owns biotechnology application and conceptual comparison; Unit 14 owns measurement principles, experimental evidence, instrumentation, statistical interpretation and ethical governance. Unit 3 owns molecular mechanisms, Unit 4 immunology, Unit 5 development, Unit 8 inheritance, Unit 10 ecology, Unit 11 algorithms and Unit 12 industrial engineering. Preview later methods when needed without duplicating full chapters.
- Use benign educational examples, abstract models and published high-level evidence. No actionable pathogen culture or engineering, harmful genetic modification, production recipes, invasive experimentation, radioisotope handling or medical treatment instructions. Research involving biological hazards, animals, humans or radiation requires trained personnel and authorised oversight.
- Verify current laws, biosafety rules, regulatory bodies and journal AI policies at actual publication; distinguish prediction from experimental or clinical validation. Maintain provenance and research integrity.
