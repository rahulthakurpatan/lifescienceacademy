# Life Science Academy — Subunit 1.D: Enzymes and Catalysis

Understand biological catalysis through enzyme properties, catalytic mechanisms, quantitative kinetics, inhibition and regulation of enzyme activity.

Five approved learning nodes. See [Unit 1 master map](unit-01-structure-function-biomolecules-subunit-map.md) for source references and the original A–J crosswalk.

## Node contracts

### 1.D.01 — Enzyme Fundamentals and Catalytic Mechanisms

**Student-facing description:** Classification, specificity, active sites, cofactors, coenzymes, transition states and major catalytic mechanisms.

**Core scope:** Enzyme classes including translocases, active sites, specificity, apo/holoenzymes, cofactors, prosthetic groups, induced fit, transition-state stabilisation, acid–base, covalent, metal-ion and proximity/orientation catalysis with representative benign enzymes.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret catalytic energy diagrams and distinguish changes in activation energy from equilibrium changes.

**Progression:** Build on preceding biomolecular concepts. Prepare 1.D.02.

### 1.D.02 — Michaelis–Menten Kinetics and Quantitative Analysis

**Student-facing description:** Derivation, steady-state assumptions, Km, Vmax, kcat, catalytic efficiency and kinetic plots.

**Core scope:** Initial-rate framework, enzyme–substrate complex, steady-state derivation, parameter units, saturation, turnover, catalytic efficiency, linear transformations and nonlinear fitting concepts; distinguish Km from Kd.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Derive and apply the rate equation, estimate parameters and compare limitations of graphical methods.

**Progression:** Build on 1.D.01. Prepare 1.D.03.

### 1.D.03 — Enzyme Inhibition and Activity Modulation

**Student-facing description:** Competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive, mixed and irreversible inhibition, inhibition constants and graphical interpretation.

**Core scope:** Reversible and irreversible inhibition, binding-state models, apparent Km and Vmax changes, pure noncompetitive as special mixed case, Ki, effects of substrate concentration and time, graphical and data-based inference.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Identify inhibition patterns from supplied kinetics, solve standard parameter problems and recognise ambiguous evidence.

**Progression:** Build on 1.D.02. Prepare 1.D.04.

### 1.D.04 — Multisubstrate and Allosteric Enzyme Kinetics

**Student-facing description:** Sequential and ping-pong mechanisms, cooperative binding, Hill equation, MWC and KNF models.

**Core scope:** Ordered/random sequential and ping-pong mechanisms, initial-rate plot patterns, homotropic and heterotropic interactions, sigmoidal rates, Hill coefficient and limitations, concerted and sequential conformational models.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret multisubstrate and cooperative plots, compare allosteric models and avoid interpreting Hill slope as exact site number.

**Progression:** Build on 1.D.03. Prepare 1.D.05.

### 1.D.05 — Enzyme Regulation and Isozymes

**Student-facing description:** Feedback inhibition, covalent modification, phosphorylation, zymogens, enzyme abundance, isozymes and metabolic regulation.

**Core scope:** Allosteric regulation versus catalysis, feedback and feedforward, reversible covalent changes, proteolytic activation, enzyme synthesis/degradation, localisation, isozyme differences and integrated pathway control.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret activity, abundance and regulatory data, compare rapid and slow regulation and relate isozymes to biological roles.

**Progression:** Build on 1.D.04. Bridge to subunit 1.E.
