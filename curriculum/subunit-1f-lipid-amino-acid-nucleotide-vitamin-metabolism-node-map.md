# Life Science Academy — Subunit 1.F: Lipid, Amino Acid, Nucleotide and Vitamin Metabolism

Integrate the biosynthesis, degradation and regulation of lipids, amino acids, nucleotides and vitamins within the broader framework of cellular metabolic homeostasis.

Five approved learning nodes. See [Unit 1 master map](unit-01-structure-function-biomolecules-subunit-map.md) for source references and the original A–J crosswalk.

## Node contracts

### 1.F.01 — Fatty Acid and Ketone Body Metabolism

**Student-facing description:** Fatty acid activation, carnitine shuttle, beta oxidation, fatty acid biosynthesis, ketogenesis, ketolysis and regulation.

**Core scope:** Activation and transport, beta-oxidation steps, even/odd-chain and unsaturated fatty acids, energetic accounting, acetyl-CoA carboxylase, fatty acid synthase, malonyl-CoA, NADPH, elongation/desaturation, reciprocal control, ketone formation/utilisation.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Calculate fatty-acid energy yields with stated P/O assumptions and compare synthesis and oxidation.

**Progression:** Build on preceding biomolecular concepts. Prepare 1.F.02.

### 1.F.02 — Complex Lipid and Cholesterol Metabolism

**Student-facing description:** Triacylglycerols, phospholipids, sphingolipids, cholesterol, lipoproteins, bile acids and eicosanoid metabolism.

**Core scope:** TAG synthesis and mobilisation, glycerol and acyl group metabolism, phospholipid/sphingolipid synthesis and turnover, cholesterol pathway and HMG-CoA reductase regulation, lipid transport, bile acids and eicosanoid formation at NET/SET scope.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Interpret pathway maps and lipid transport/regulatory data, compare metabolic fates of lipid classes.

**Progression:** Build on 1.F.01. Prepare 1.F.03.

### 1.F.03 — Amino Acid Metabolism and Nitrogen Homeostasis

**Student-facing description:** Transamination, deamination, ammonia transport, urea cycle, amino acid biosynthesis, degradation and carbon skeleton metabolism.

**Core scope:** PLP-dependent transamination, oxidative deamination, glutamine/alanine transport, ammonia disposal, urea cycle and energetic cost, glucogenic/ketogenic amino acids, biosynthetic families, essential amino acids and links to central metabolism; plant nitrogen assimilation in Unit 6.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Trace nitrogen and carbon, calculate urea-cycle costs and classify amino-acid metabolic entry points.

**Progression:** Build on 1.F.02. Prepare 1.F.04.

### 1.F.04 — Purine and Pyrimidine Nucleotide Metabolism

**Student-facing description:** De novo synthesis, salvage pathways, nucleotide degradation, deoxyribonucleotide synthesis and metabolic regulation.

**Core scope:** PRPP, de novo purine synthesis to IMP and AMP/GMP, purine salvage and catabolism, pyrimidine ring synthesis and UMP/CTP formation, ribonucleotide reductase, dTMP and folate coupling, pyrimidine degradation and feedback control.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Compare purine and pyrimidine synthesis, trace precursor contributions and interpret pathway-regulation examples.

**Progression:** Build on 1.F.03. Prepare 1.F.05.

### 1.F.05 — Vitamins, Coenzymes and Integrated Metabolic Regulation

**Student-facing description:** Vitamin structures, biological functions, active forms, coenzyme chemistry, vitamin metabolism, deficiency concepts and integrated metabolic pathways.

**Core scope:** Water- and fat-soluble vitamins, structures and active derivatives, coenzyme participation in reaction types, absorption/transport and conversion concepts as relevant, deficiency-related biochemical mechanisms, integration of carbohydrate, lipid, amino-acid and nucleotide metabolism and tissue-specific fed/fasted regulation; avoid medical dosing advice.

**Evidence and reasoning:** Map vitamins to coenzyme-dependent reactions, interpret biochemical deficiency patterns and explain coordinated pathway changes.

**Progression:** Build on 1.F.04. Integrate Unit 1 and bridge to Unit 2.
