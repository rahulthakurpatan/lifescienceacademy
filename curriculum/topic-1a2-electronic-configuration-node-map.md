## Chronological learning sequence

### I. From spectra to the quantum picture

*Educational purpose:* Why atomic electrons require quantized, wave-based descriptions instead of fixed planetary tracks.

#### 1.A.2.01 — Atomic Spectra and the Need for Electron Configuration

- **Learning question:** Why did experiments force scientists to reconsider the arrangement of electrons?
- **Full-note content contract:** Link Node 1.A.1's nuclear/electron-cloud model to discrete absorption and emission spectra; energy-level transitions, evidence versus model, distinction between spectra and electron configuration.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Hydrogen line spectrum with selected discrete transitions; interpret why line positions reveal discrete energy differences.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not repeat Rutherford/Bohr history in full; distinguish an energy-level diagram from a literal electron path.
- **State:** Published on GitHub main (2026-11-21). Complete article: [Read Node 1.A.2.01](https://rahulthakurpatan.github.io/lifescienceacademy/notes/unit-01/subunit-1a/topic-1a2/node-01-atomic-spectra-need-electron-configuration.html).

#### 1.A.2.02 — Quantized Light: Wavelength, Frequency and Photon Energy

- **Learning question:** How can a colour or wavelength quantify an electron's energy change?
- **Full-note content contract:** Electromagnetic wavelength/frequency, c=λν, E=hν=hc/λ, electronvolt vs joule, absorption/emission and meaningful spectral calculations.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Convert 500 nm to photon energy; solve several emission/absorption energy problems with units.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Avoid treating photon wavelength as orbital size or ionization energy without the actual transition.
- **State:** Published on GitHub main (2026-11-21). Full chapter: [Read Node 1.A.2.02](https://rahulthakurpatan.github.io/lifescienceacademy/notes/unit-01/subunit-1a/topic-1a2/node-02-quantized-light-wavelength-frequency-photon-energy.html).

#### 1.A.2.03 — Matter Waves: de Broglie Wavelength and Electron Behaviour

- **Learning question:** Why can the electron behave like a wave while still being measured as a particle?
- **Full-note content contract:** Particle momentum and λ=h/p, electron diffraction evidence, wave-particle complementarity, standing-wave motivation and nonrelativistic examples.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Compute de Broglie wavelength for a given electron momentum; discuss electron diffraction and limitation of classical orbits.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Keep scattering/instrumentation details within scope; no advanced solid-state band calculations.
- **State:** Published on GitHub main (2026-11-21). Read the full chapter: [Node 1.A.2.03 — Matter Waves](https://rahulthakurpatan.github.io/lifescienceacademy/notes/unit-01/subunit-1a/topic-1a2/node-03-matter-waves-de-broglie-electron-behaviour.html).

#### 1.A.2.04 — Heisenberg Uncertainty and the Failure of Fixed Orbits

- **Learning question:** Why can position and momentum not both be exactly specified for an electron?
- **Full-note content contract:** ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, uncertainty as quantum principle rather than instrument defect, momentum/position trade-off, classical-orbit limitations, sample dimensionally checked estimates.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Use a stated position uncertainty to infer a lower bound on momentum uncertainty; distinguish uncertainty from probabilistic ignorance.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not say an electron has zero position information or that the uncertainty principle causes electrons to orbit.
- **State:** Published on GitHub main (2026-11-21). Read the complete textbook chapter: [Node 1.A.2.04 — Heisenberg Uncertainty](https://rahulthakurpatan.github.io/lifescienceacademy/notes/unit-01/subunit-1a/topic-1a2/node-04-heisenberg-uncertainty-failure-fixed-orbits.html).

#### 1.A.2.05 — Quantum Orbitals: Wavefunctions and Probability Density

- **Learning question:** What replaces an exact electron orbit in the quantum-mechanical picture?
- **Full-note content contract:** Schrödinger-model motivation; orbit versus orbital, ψ, |ψ|² probability density, normalization, nodal concept, probability clouds and interpretation boundaries.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Describe a probability density diagram; explain why orbitals are mathematical states and not rigid containers.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not solve the full Schrödinger differential equation; keep wavefunction mathematics appropriately qualitative.
- **State:** Published on GitHub main (2026-11-21). Full notes: [Node 1.A.2.05 — Quantum Orbitals and Probability](https://rahulthakurpatan.github.io/lifescienceacademy/notes/unit-01/subunit-1a/topic-1a2/node-05-quantum-orbitals-wavefunctions-probability-density.html).

### II. Quantum numbers and orbital architecture

*Educational purpose:* How shells, subshells, orbital orientations, spin and electron capacity emerge from permitted quantum states.

#### 1.A.2.06 — Principal Quantum Number n: Shells, Scale and Energy

- **Learning question:** What does n tell us about a permitted electronic state?
- **Full-note content contract:** n=1,2,...; K/L/M/N historical shell labels, size trends for hydrogen-like states, hydrogenic energy dependence, shell identification and allowed occupancy idea.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Identify shell for n=1..4, compare hydrogen-like energy and size qualitatively, interpret level transition.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Electron capacity formula is formally consolidated in Node 09; no false universal radius for multi-electron atoms.
- **State:** Published on GitHub main (2026-11-22). Complete original textbook chapter: [Read Node 1.A.2.06](https://rahulthakurpatan.github.io/lifescienceacademy/notes/unit-01/subunit-1a/topic-1a2/node-06-principal-quantum-number-shells-scale-energy.html).

#### 1.A.2.07 — Azimuthal Quantum Number l: Subshells and Angular Momentum

- **Learning question:** How do subshells differ within one principal shell?
- **Full-note content contract:** l=0...(n−1); s,p,d,f correspondence; allowed n-l combinations; relation to orbital angular momentum magnitude, possible shapes and angular dependence.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** List allowed l for n=2,3,4 and name the subshells; distinguish impossible 2d and 1p.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Detailed shapes and nodes are developed in Node 10 to avoid premature memorization.
- **State:** Published on GitHub main (2026-11-22). Full textbook chapter: [Read Node 1.A.2.07](https://rahulthakurpatan.github.io/lifescienceacademy/notes/unit-01/subunit-1a/topic-1a2/node-07-azimuthal-quantum-number-subshells-angular-momentum.html).

#### 1.A.2.08 — Magnetic and Spin Quantum Numbers: m_l and m_s

- **Learning question:** How can orbitals of a subshell and two spin states be distinguished?
- **Full-note content contract:** m_l=−l,...,+l; degeneracy, orbital orientation labels, intrinsic electron spin m_s=±1/2; spin does not mean a tiny ball literally rotates.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Enumerate m_l for p and d subshells; show spin-up/down conceptual arrows in orbital diagrams.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not claim orbital orientation always predicts an immutable direction in complex atoms or fields.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.09 — Allowed Quantum Numbers, Orbital Counts and Electron Capacity

- **Learning question:** How many distinct orbital and electron states are permitted?
- **Full-note content contract:** Combine (n,l,m_l,m_s) restrictions; count 2l+1 orbitals/subshell and n² orbitals/shell, max 2(2l+1) electrons per subshell and 2n² electrons/shell; valid/invalid sets.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Derive 3p→3 orbitals→6 electrons, n=3→9 orbitals→18 electrons; test many candidate quantum-number tuples.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Capacities are upper bounds for available states, not a claim that every ground-state n-shell fills before next shell begins.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.10 — Orbital Shapes and Nodes: s, p, d and f

- **Learning question:** What are the spatial patterns behind orbital names?
- **Full-note content contract:** spherical s density, dumbbell-like p lobes, qualitative d and f shapes; nodes versus empty space, radial/ angular nodes for hydrogenic orbitals (n−l−1 and l), probability vs boundary surfaces.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Interpret labelled 1s,2s,2p,3d probability schematics and calculate allowed radial/angular node counts.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not present orbitals as fixed hard shells or give exact shape claims for strongly interacting many-electron systems.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

### III. Orbital energies and filling principles

*Educational purpose:* Why orbital energies differ, and how ground-state electron configurations are constructed and checked.

#### 1.A.2.11 — Orbital Energies in Hydrogen and Many-Electron Atoms

- **Learning question:** Why does energy ordering differ between a one-electron atom and a multi-electron atom?
- **Full-note content contract:** Hydrogen-like energy mainly n-dependent in basic Coulomb model; degeneracy, subshell splitting with additional electrons; general factors affecting energy, electronic ground/excited states.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Compare 2s/2p in hydrogen-like versus many-electron atoms conceptually; energy ordering diagram.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Avoid treating one fixed 4s-versus-3d energy sequence as universally true for all atoms and ions.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.12 — Shielding, Penetration and Effective Nuclear Charge

- **Learning question:** Why do different subshells experience the nucleus differently?
- **Full-note content contract:** Core shielding, penetration, effective nuclear charge Z_eff, trends within shell, qualitative orbital stabilization, interpretation of s/p/d radial probability and energy.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Rank penetration s>p>d>f in comparable shells; distinguish nuclear charge Z from experienced effective attraction.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Detailed periodic trends belong in Topic 1.A.3; this node provides their electronic prerequisites only.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.13 — Aufbau Principle and the (n + l) Filling Guide

- **Learning question:** How do we predict approximate neutral ground-state electron filling order?
- **Full-note content contract:** Aufbau as energy-building guide, diagonal ordering (n+l then lower n), 1s→2s→2p→3s→3p→4s→3d..., shell/subshell capacities, exceptions and contextual limits.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Construct an Aufbau chart and trace orbital filling up to 4p using n+l; identify why 4s can be filled before 3d in neutral atoms.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** State n+l as a useful rule of thumb, not a rigorous universal energy law.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.14 — Pauli Exclusion Principle and Electron Pairing

- **Learning question:** Why can only two electrons occupy one spatial orbital in the simple configuration model?
- **Full-note content contract:** Four-quantum-number distinctness, fermion antisymmetry conceptual basis, opposite spin arrows in one spatial orbital, filling limits and impossible diagrams.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Diagnose orbital boxes with two identical spins in same orbital, contrast orbital occupancy and spin orientation.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Avoid unsupported assertion that spin is literal clockwise/counterclockwise spinning charge.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.15 — Hund's Rule and Ground-State Orbital Diagrams

- **Learning question:** Why do equal-energy orbitals fill singly before pairing?
- **Full-note content contract:** Degenerate orbitals, Hund maximum multiplicity for relevant cases, consistent parallel spins, pairing vs promotion, orbital-box notation, conceptual exchange/correlation.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Draw p²,p³,p⁴,p⁵, d⁵ boxes, count unpaired electrons and identify ground-state arrangements.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not claim exchange energy alone is a universally complete many-electron explanation.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.16 — Write Ground-State Configurations from Hydrogen to Calcium

- **Learning question:** Can we construct and verify configurations for main-group elements?
- **Full-note content contract:** Step-by-step H–Ca (Z=1–20), subshell notation, noble-gas shorthand [He],[Ne],[Ar], count check, common oxygen/sodium/magnesium/chlorine examples.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Write C,N,O,Na,Mg,Al,S,Cl,Ar,K,Ca; compare orbital boxes and verify electron count.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not conflate filling order with ion electron-removal order, handled in Node 19.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

### IV. Configurations, ions and biological relevance

*Educational purpose:* How neutral/ionic configurations, exceptions, spin and biological elements connect the rules to real science.

#### 1.A.2.17 — Transition-Metal Configurations Sc–Zn and Noble-Gas Shorthand

- **Learning question:** How are the 3d and 4s electrons distributed in the first transition series?
- **Full-note content contract:** Neutral Sc–Zn 3d/4s configurations, [Ar] core, ground-state notation, distinguishing final-electron order from chemical reactivity, qualitative transition metal context.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Determine Fe, Co, Ni, Zn configurations and electron counts; label core and 3d/4s contributions.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Cr/Cu anomalies are examined independently in Node 18, not hidden as mere memorization.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.18 — Chromium, Copper and the Limits of Simple Filling Rules

- **Learning question:** Why are some experimentally established configurations different from naive Aufbau predictions?
- **Full-note content contract:** Cr [Ar]3d⁵4s¹, Cu [Ar]3d¹⁰4s¹; energy competition, limits of simplistic half-filled/full-filled slogan; known contexts and ground-state nuance.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Compare naive predictions with measured Cr/Cu configurations; identify why predictions require evidence and subtle energy considerations.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not claim all half-filled subshells always have the same exception or force one rule onto ions.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.19 — Electronic Configurations of Ions and the 4s–3d Issue

- **Learning question:** How does electron loss or gain change configuration?
- **Full-note content contract:** Cation/anion electron counts, main-group octet examples, transition-metal oxidation states, removing 4s electrons before 3d when forming common transition-metal cations from neutral atoms, Fe²⁺/Fe³⁺, Cu⁺/Cu²⁺.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Write Na⁺, Mg²⁺, O²⁻, Cl⁻, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺, Cu⁺; verify Z, charge and electron count.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** No rigid blanket statement that 4s is always higher or lower in energy; contextualize atom/ion differences.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.20 — Core versus Valence Electrons: Main-Group and Transition Metals

- **Learning question:** Which electrons contribute to chemistry and why is valence counting context-dependent?
- **Full-note content contract:** Core-valence boundary; outer-shell ns/np for main group; d-electron participation for transition metals, group/electron configuration correlations, ionic charge preview.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Identify core and valence for C, Na, Mg, Cl, Fe under stated conventions; distinguish shell from chemical valence.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Periodic trends are in 1.A.3 and bond mechanism in 1.A.4: keep only the electronic prerequisites here.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.21 — Unpaired Electrons, Electron Spin and Magnetic Response

- **Learning question:** How can electron configuration predict a first approximation to magnetic behaviour?
- **Full-note content contract:** Paired versus unpaired electrons, atom/ion paramagnetism and diamagnetism, simple spin counts, scope limits in molecules and complexes, caution about molecular O₂.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Determine unpaired electrons for N, O, Fe³⁺ free-ion in context; compare paramagnetic versus diamagnetic species.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Do not infer O₂ molecular magnetism solely from isolated oxygen atomic configurations; molecular orbitals and ligand fields need separate models.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).

#### 1.A.2.22 — Electronic Configuration in Biology and Integrated CSIR-NET Practice

- **Learning question:** Why do these quantum rules matter for living cells and biological ions?
- **Full-note content contract:** Na⁺/K⁺ in electrochemical physiology, Mg²⁺ and Ca²⁺, Fe/Cu electronic arrangements and redox state implications, qualitative coordination relevance, integrated electron-count/orbital/magnetism problems and bridge to periodic properties.
- **Essential visual / worked practice:** Multi-step worked cases for K⁺, Ca²⁺, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺ and Cu ions; explain evidence versus inference and solve original NET/SET-style integrated questions.
- **Boundary and scientific caveat:** Avoid deep physiological mechanism, periodic trends or bonding geometry: these belong to their later topics.
- **State:** Planned (no published URL).
