## Complete menu with English descriptions

### 1.A — Chemical and Biophysical Foundations

Establish the chemical and physicochemical foundations of living systems, connecting atomic structure, molecular interactions, biological solutions, reaction kinetics and thermodynamics.

- 1.A.01 — **Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding:** Atoms, isotopes, ions, electronic configuration, orbitals, periodic properties, chemical bonds, hybridisation, resonance and molecular geometry.
- 1.A.02 — **Water and Molecular Stabilising Interactions:** Water properties, polarity, hydration, electrostatic interactions, hydrogen bonds, van der Waals forces and hydrophobic effects.
- 1.A.03 — **Biological Solutions, pH and Buffer Systems:** Concentration units, colligative properties, osmotic pressure, acids, bases, pKa, titration curves, Henderson–Hasselbalch equation and biological buffers.
- 1.A.04 — **Chemical Kinetics and Reaction Dynamics:** Reaction rates, rate laws, reaction order, molecularity, half-life, activation energy and Arrhenius equation.
- 1.A.05 — **Thermodynamics, Chemical Equilibrium and Redox Principles:** Thermodynamic laws, enthalpy, entropy, Gibbs free energy, equilibrium constants, redox potentials and Nernst equation.

### 1.B — Carbohydrates, Lipids and Nucleic Acids

Explore the composition, chemical properties, structural organisation, stability and biological functions of carbohydrates, lipids and nucleic acids.

- 1.B.01 — **Carbohydrate Chemistry and Structural Organisation:** Monosaccharides, stereochemistry, anomers, epimers, mutarotation, reducing sugars, disaccharides, polysaccharides and complex carbohydrates.
- 1.B.02 — **Lipid Chemistry and Biological Organisation:** Fatty acids, neutral lipids, phospholipids, glycolipids, sphingolipids, cholesterol, steroids, eicosanoids, micelles and lipid bilayers.
- 1.B.03 — **Nucleotide Chemistry and DNA Conformation:** Bases, nucleosides, nucleotides, phosphodiester bonds, double helix, base pairing and A-, B- and Z-DNA.
- 1.B.04 — **RNA Structure and Functional Conformations:** RNA folding, secondary and tertiary structures, hairpins, pseudoknots, mRNA, tRNA, rRNA, regulatory RNAs and ribozymes.
- 1.B.05 — **Nucleic Acid Topology and Molecular Stability:** Linking number, twist, writhe, supercoiling, base stacking, melting temperature, denaturation, renaturation and hybridisation.

### 1.C — Amino Acids and Protein Structure

Trace protein organisation from amino acid chemistry and peptide formation to higher-order conformations, folding, stability and biological functions.

- 1.C.01 — **Amino Acid Chemistry and Peptide Formation:** Amino acid classification, stereochemistry, zwitterions, pKa, isoelectric point, titration and peptide bonds.
- 1.C.02 — **Protein Backbone Conformation and Secondary Structure:** Primary structure, peptide planarity, phi and psi angles, Ramachandran plots, alpha helices, beta sheets, turns and loops.
- 1.C.03 — **Protein Tertiary and Quaternary Organisation:** Motifs, domains, folds, structural classification, subunit interactions, protein assemblies and stabilising forces.
- 1.C.04 — **Protein Folding, Stability and Denaturation:** Folding energetics, Anfinsen's experiment, chaperones, folding landscapes, denaturation, renaturation and aggregation.
- 1.C.05 — **Protein Structure–Function Relationships:** Fibrous and globular proteins, collagen, keratin, myoglobin, haemoglobin, ligand binding, cooperativity and allostery.

### 1.D — Enzymes and Catalysis

Understand biological catalysis through enzyme properties, catalytic mechanisms, quantitative kinetics, inhibition and regulation of enzyme activity.

- 1.D.01 — **Enzyme Fundamentals and Catalytic Mechanisms:** Classification, specificity, active sites, cofactors, coenzymes, transition states and major catalytic mechanisms.
- 1.D.02 — **Michaelis–Menten Kinetics and Quantitative Analysis:** Derivation, steady-state assumptions, Km, Vmax, kcat, catalytic efficiency and kinetic plots.
- 1.D.03 — **Enzyme Inhibition and Activity Modulation:** Competitive, noncompetitive, uncompetitive, mixed and irreversible inhibition, inhibition constants and graphical interpretation.
- 1.D.04 — **Multisubstrate and Allosteric Enzyme Kinetics:** Sequential and ping-pong mechanisms, cooperative binding, Hill equation, MWC and KNF models.
- 1.D.05 — **Enzyme Regulation and Isozymes:** Feedback inhibition, covalent modification, phosphorylation, zymogens, enzyme abundance, isozymes and metabolic regulation.

### 1.E — Bioenergetics and Carbohydrate Metabolism

Explain cellular energy conversion and carbohydrate metabolism through interconnected biochemical pathways, respiratory mechanisms and metabolic regulation.

- 1.E.01 — **Bioenergetics and Biological Energy Transduction:** ATP, coupled reactions, group-transfer potential, high-energy compounds, biological electron carriers and energy conversion.
- 1.E.02 — **Glycolysis, Pyruvate Metabolism and Fermentation:** Glycolytic reactions, energetics, regulation, aerobic and anaerobic metabolism, pyruvate dehydrogenase and acetyl-CoA formation.
- 1.E.03 — **Citric Acid Cycle and Metabolic Integration:** TCA cycle reactions, regulation, energetic yield, amphibolic functions and anaplerotic reactions.
- 1.E.04 — **Electron Transport and Oxidative Phosphorylation:** Respiratory complexes, chemiosmosis, proton motive force, ATP synthase, respiratory control, inhibitors, uncouplers and mitochondrial shuttles.
- 1.E.05 — **Glucose Homeostasis and Alternative Carbohydrate Pathways:** Gluconeogenesis, glycogenesis, glycogenolysis, Cori cycle, pentose phosphate pathway, fructose and galactose metabolism.

### 1.F — Lipid, Amino Acid, Nucleotide and Vitamin Metabolism

Integrate the biosynthesis, degradation and regulation of lipids, amino acids, nucleotides and vitamins within the broader framework of cellular metabolic homeostasis.

- 1.F.01 — **Fatty Acid and Ketone Body Metabolism:** Fatty acid activation, carnitine shuttle, beta oxidation, fatty acid biosynthesis, ketogenesis, ketolysis and regulation.
- 1.F.02 — **Complex Lipid and Cholesterol Metabolism:** Triacylglycerols, phospholipids, sphingolipids, cholesterol, lipoproteins, bile acids and eicosanoid metabolism.
- 1.F.03 — **Amino Acid Metabolism and Nitrogen Homeostasis:** Transamination, deamination, ammonia transport, urea cycle, amino acid biosynthesis, degradation and carbon skeleton metabolism.
- 1.F.04 — **Purine and Pyrimidine Nucleotide Metabolism:** De novo synthesis, salvage pathways, nucleotide degradation, deoxyribonucleotide synthesis and metabolic regulation.
- 1.F.05 — **Vitamins, Coenzymes and Integrated Metabolic Regulation:** Vitamin structures, biological functions, active forms, coenzyme chemistry, vitamin metabolism, deficiency concepts and integrated metabolic pathways.

## Learning progression

Chemical and physical foundations → carbohydrate, lipid and nucleic-acid structure → amino acids and proteins → enzymes and regulation → energy conversion and carbohydrate metabolism → integrated lipid, nitrogen, nucleotide and vitamin metabolism. This sequence replaces the retired fragmented architecture.
