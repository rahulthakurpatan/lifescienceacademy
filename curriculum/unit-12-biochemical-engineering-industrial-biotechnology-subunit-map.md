## Subunit descriptions and learning sequences

### 12.A — Introductory Mathematics

Develop mathematical foundations for biochemical engineering through calculus, differential equations, linear algebra and numerical solutions of biological and engineering problems.

- 12.A.01 — **Mathematical Foundations and Differential Calculus:** Functions, limits, continuity, exponential and logarithmic functions, differentiation, partial derivatives, chain rule, Taylor series, maxima, minima and biological rate calculations.
- 12.A.02 — **Integral Calculus and Engineering Applications:** Indefinite and definite integration, substitution, integration by parts, multiple integrals, accumulated quantities and applications in biological processes.
- 12.A.03 — **First-Order Ordinary Differential Equations:** Order and degree, initial conditions, separable and first-order linear equations, integrating factors, growth, decay and reaction kinetics models.
- 12.A.04 — **Second and Higher-Order Differential Equations:** Homogeneous and non-homogeneous equations, characteristic equations, complementary and particular solutions, boundary conditions, Laplace transforms and oscillatory systems.
- 12.A.05 — **Linear Algebra and Matrix-Based Biological Analysis:** Vectors, matrices, determinants, rank, inverse, simultaneous equations, Gaussian elimination, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and systems of differential equations.
- 12.A.06 — **Numerical Methods and Computational Problem Solving:** Numerical errors, convergence, bisection, Newton–Raphson, interpolation, numerical differentiation, trapezoidal and Simpson's rules, Euler and Runge–Kutta methods.

### 12.B — Engineering Principles

Establish physical and engineering principles for biological production through material and energy balances, substance properties, fluid motion, heat and mass transfer and transport equipment.

- 12.B.01 — **Material Properties, Engineering Units and Process Fundamentals:** SI units, dimensional analysis, density, specific gravity, viscosity, pressure, concentration, ideal and real gases, phase behaviour and heat capacity.
- 12.B.02 — **Material Balance and Process Stoichiometry:** Mass conservation, system boundaries, steady and unsteady-state balances, reacting and non-reacting systems, limiting reactants, conversion, yield, selectivity, recycle, bypass and purge.
- 12.B.03 — **Energy Balance and Thermal Process Calculations:** Internal energy, enthalpy, sensible and latent heat, heat of reaction, steady and unsteady-state energy balances and integrated mass–energy calculations.
- 12.B.04 — **Momentum Transfer and Fluid Mechanics:** Newton's law of viscosity, laminar and turbulent flow, Reynolds number, continuity, Bernoulli and Hagen–Poiseuille equations, pressure drop and frictional losses.
- 12.B.05 — **Heat Transfer and Thermal Engineering:** Conduction, convection, radiation, Fourier's law, Newton's law of cooling, thermal resistance, overall heat-transfer coefficients, heat exchangers and log mean temperature difference.
- 12.B.06 — **Mass Transfer and Separation Equipment:** Molecular diffusion, Fick's laws, convective and interphase mass transfer, coefficients, Sherwood and Schmidt numbers, packed towers, plate columns and gas–liquid contactors.

### 12.C — Thermodynamics in Biological Systems

Extend classical thermodynamics to living open systems through irreversibility, forces, fluxes, entropy production, reaction coupling, biological energy conversion and oscillations.

- 12.C.01 — **Classical Thermodynamics and Biological Energy Principles:** First and second laws, internal energy, enthalpy, entropy, Gibbs and Helmholtz free energies, chemical potential, equilibrium and reversibility.
- 12.C.02 — **Living Systems as Open Non-Equilibrium Systems:** Open, closed and isolated systems, exchange of matter and energy, equilibrium versus steady state, dissipative systems, irreversibility and limits of equilibrium descriptions.
- 12.C.03 — **Thermodynamic Forces, Fluxes and Entropy Production:** Chemical potential, temperature and concentration gradients, conjugate forces and fluxes, entropy balance, entropy production, constitutive equations and Onsager reciprocity.
- 12.C.04 — **Thermodynamics of Coupled Biochemical Reactions:** Exergonic and endergonic reactions, reaction affinity, ATP coupling, phosphorylation potential, chemical and transport coupling, coupling coefficients and efficiency.
- 12.C.05 — **Thermodynamic Analysis of Oxidative Phosphorylation:** Redox potentials, electrochemical gradients, proton motive force, membrane potential, chemiosmotic coupling, ATP synthesis, proton leakage and energy-conversion efficiency.
- 12.C.06 — **Glycolytic Oscillations and Biological Clocks:** Nonlinear dynamics, positive and negative feedback, glycolytic oscillations, phosphofructokinase regulation, limit cycles, dissipative structures, circadian oscillations and entrainment.

### 12.D — Bioprocess Engineering and Technology

Explain upstream bioprocess fundamentals through microbial growth, substrate use, reactor configurations, asepsis, balances, oxygen transfer, industrial fermentation and economic evaluation.

- 12.D.01 — **Microbial Growth and Industrial Culture Fundamentals:** Microbial growth phases, biomass estimation, specific growth rate, generation time, nutrients, industrial production organisms, media components, growth factors and growth-associated products.
- 12.D.02 — **Microbial Growth Kinetics and Substrate Utilisation:** Monod kinetics, substrate saturation, inhibition, biomass and product yields, maintenance, Pirt and Luedeking–Piret models and substrate consumption.
- 12.D.03 — **Bioreactor Design and Operating Configurations:** Stirred-tank, airlift, bubble-column, packed and fluidised beds, batch, fed-batch, continuous, plug-flow and enzyme reactors, chemostats, turbidostats, dilution and washout.
- 12.D.04 — **Sterilisation and Aseptic Bioprocess Technology:** Thermal inactivation concepts, D-, Z- and F-values, batch and continuous sterilisation principles, media and air filtration, contamination control, aseptic design and CIP/SIP.
- 12.D.05 — **Material and Energy Balances in Microbial Processes:** Growth stoichiometry, elemental balances, respiratory quotient, oxygen demand, biomass and product yields, degree of reduction, metabolic heat and cooling requirements.
- 12.D.06 — **Oxygen Transfer, Aeration, Agitation and Fluid Rheology:** Dissolved oxygen, oxygen uptake and transfer, volumetric transfer coefficient, oxygen limitation, mixing, power and Newtonian/non-Newtonian broth rheology.
- 12.D.07 — **Industrial Fermentation and Microbial Product Technology:** Compare industrial production principles and process flow sheets for antibiotics, organic acids, alcohols, vitamins, enzymes, bioplastics and microbial steroid biotransformation.
- 12.D.08 — **Integrated Fermentation Processes and Process Economics:** Integrate upstream and downstream flow sheets, titre, yield, productivity, raw materials, utilities, capital and operating costs and industrial feasibility.

### 12.E — Enzymes and Microbial Technology

Explore industrial biocatalysis through enzyme properties, alternative reaction media, protein engineering, immobilisation, reactor kinetics and diffusion limitations.

- 12.E.01 — **Industrial Enzymes and Fundamentals of Biocatalysis:** Microbial enzymes, industrial biocatalysts, activity, specificity, stability, catalytic efficiency, cofactors, enzyme selection and industrial applications.
- 12.E.02 — **Enzymes in Organic Solvents and Ionic Liquids:** Non-aqueous enzymology, water activity, solvent polarity, organic solvent effects, ionic liquids, biphasic reactions, selectivity and limitations.
- 12.E.03 — **Enzyme Engineering and Protein Engineering Strategies:** Structure–function relationships, random mutagenesis, directed evolution, rational and semi-rational design, screening and computational approaches to activity, specificity and stability.
- 12.E.04 — **Immobilisation of Enzymes and Whole Cells:** Adsorption, covalent attachment, entrapment, encapsulation, cross-linking, carrier materials, whole-cell immobilisation, recovery, reuse and stability.
- 12.E.05 — **Immobilised Enzyme Kinetics and Reactor Engineering:** Free versus immobilised kinetics, apparent Michaelis–Menten parameters, batch and continuous reactors, packed/fluidised beds, residence time, conversion and deactivation.
- 12.E.06 — **Diffusional Resistance, Thiele Modulus and Effectiveness Factor:** External and internal mass-transfer resistance, diffusion in porous matrices, reaction–diffusion equations, Thiele modulus, effectiveness factor and concentration profiles.

### 12.F — Downstream Processing in Biotechnology

Understand biological product recovery from biomass separation and cellular disruption through isolation, purification, concentration, drying and integrated recovery evaluation.

- 12.F.01 — **Biomass Removal and Cell Disruption Technologies:** Product location, sedimentation, flocculation, filtration, centrifugation, mechanical and non-mechanical disruption, homogenisation and bead milling.
- 12.F.02 — **Product Isolation by Precipitation and Extraction:** Salting-in/out, ammonium sulfate, isoelectric and solvent precipitation, partition coefficients, liquid–liquid, aqueous two-phase and supercritical extraction.
- 12.F.03 — **Membrane-Based Separation and Purification:** Microfiltration, ultrafiltration, nanofiltration, reverse osmosis, dialysis, diafiltration, molecular-weight cut-off, flux, rejection, concentration polarisation and fouling.
- 12.F.04 — **Adsorption and Chromatographic Purification:** Adsorption isotherms, ion exchange, affinity, size exclusion, hydrophobic interaction and reverse-phase chromatography, capacity, breakthrough and resolution.
- 12.F.05 — **Product Concentration, Crystallisation and Drying:** Evaporation, concentration, crystallisation, moisture removal, drying kinetics, spray, freeze and fluidised-bed drying, stability and formulation.
- 12.F.06 — **Integrated Downstream Process Design and Recovery Analysis:** Select and sequence separation operations using flow sheets, mass balance, percentage recovery, purification fold, specific activity, cumulative yield, purity, quality and economics.

### 12.G — Bioprocess Plant Design

Integrate production requirements, equipment selection, flow sheets, scale-up and scale-down, and safe, economically sustainable plant operation.

- 12.G.01 — **Fundamentals of Bioprocess Plant Design and Process Flow Sheets:** Design basis, production capacity, process selection, block and process flow diagrams, piping and instrumentation concepts and integrated balances.
- 12.G.02 — **Bioprocess Equipment Selection and Engineering Specifications:** Bioreactors, tanks, pumps, compressors, heat exchangers, filters, centrifuges, membranes, columns, dryers, materials, sizing and utilities.
- 12.G.03 — **Bioreactor Scale-Up and Scale-Down Principles:** Laboratory, pilot and industrial scales, geometric similarity, dimensionless numbers, power per volume, tip speed, mixing and oxygen transfer criteria, gradients and scale-down models.
- 12.G.04 — **Scale-Up of Downstream Processing and Integrated Operations:** Industrial centrifugation and filtration, membrane area, column scale-up, residence time, drying, recovery, bottlenecks and upstream–downstream coordination.
- 12.G.05 — **Plant Layout, Process Control, Safety and Economic Evaluation:** Facility layout, utilities, sensors, process instrumentation, feedback and PID concepts, GMP, quality assurance, biosafety, waste management, investment, costs and feasibility.

### 12.H — Metabolic Engineering and Synthetic Biology

Understand cellular metabolism and designed biological systems through pathway regulation, flux analysis, synthetic circuits, industrial biosynthesis and responsible applications.

- 12.H.01 — **Foundations of Metabolic Engineering and Pathway Regulation:** Metabolic networks, pathway regulation, feedback inhibition, precursor availability, bottlenecks, pathway competition, resource allocation and engineering objectives.
- 12.H.02 — **Metabolic Flux Analysis and Pathway Optimisation:** Stoichiometric networks, isotope-based flux analysis, flux balance analysis, metabolic control analysis, constraints, theoretical yields and production trade-offs.
- 12.H.03 — **Metabolic Pathway Engineering and Industrial Cell Factories:** Pathway modification principles, metabolic redirection, expression regulation, cofactor balance, heterologous pathways, systems-level approaches and benign products such as organic acids, amino acids and bioplastics.
- 12.H.04 — **Synthetic Biology, Biological Parts and Genetic Circuit Design:** Biological parts, devices and systems, modularity, standardisation, orthogonality, promoters, regulatory elements, toggle switches, oscillatory circuits, biosensors and design–build–test–learn concepts.
- 12.H.05 — **Integrated Synthetic Biology Applications and Responsible Biomanufacturing:** Synthetic metabolic pathways, cell-free systems, biological materials, sustainable manufacturing, validation, metabolic burden, genetic stability, containment, bioethics and biosafety.

## Learning progression

Mathematics → engineering properties, balances and transport → biological thermodynamics → upstream bioprocess concepts → biocatalysis → product recovery → integrated plant design → metabolic engineering and synthetic biology. Real processes iterate between these stages; the ordering is a learning scaffold.
